# Week 7: Escape Chain Integration

## Class Goal

Students complete full escape sequence to functional guard under resistance.

## Concepts

1. Full escape sequence under pressure.
2. Recovery to functional guard.

## Memory Phrase

Survive, align, frame, recover.

## Whiteboard Content

1. Decision rule under pressure: survive, align, frame, then recover guard in order.
2. Correction rule: fix the first broken link in your escape chain before trying the next step.
3. Functional guard recovery: reaching a guard where you can defend, move, and re-attack safely.
4. Sequence standard: do not skip to movement before survival and structure are stable.
5. Recovery target: complete the chain to functional guard, not just temporary space. Do not stop after creating space.
6. Transition from Week 6: head and hip control taught top stability, this week teaches bottom recovery against stable top pressure.

## Suggested Teaching Wording

Opening: A partial escape is not complete. Functional guard is the finish line.
Teaching point: When sequence breaks, identify the first broken link and fix that step first.

### Core Script

First concept is full escape sequencing under pressure. The order matters: survive first, align your body, build frames, then recover to functional guard.

Second concept is recovery standard. You are not done when you create a little space. You are done when you return to a guard where you can defend and move safely.

Watch this rep: I stabilize safety first, align my hips and shoulders, build two functional frames, then recover to guard. I do not skip steps when I feel pressure.

Common mistake: student creates space and immediately explodes without structure. Result: top player re-pins. Correction: identify first broken link, rebuild it, then continue chain.

## Drill

Drill 11: First-Broken-Link Escape Chain (Level 2).

Setup:

1. Start in side control or mount bottom.
2. Run the full sequence: survive, align, frame, recover.
3. If the rep fails, identify first failed step immediately.
4. Replay only that failed step once with focus.
5. Restart full sequence attempt under the same pressure.
6. Increase resistance in stages after clean sequence completion.

## Drill 2

Functional Guard Confirmation.

Setup:

1. Start with bottom partner having already created space from side control (frames built, partial hip escape done).
2. Bottom partner completes recovery to a specific functional guard: closed, butterfly, or half guard.
3. Hold the recovered guard stable for 5 seconds while top partner applies light re-pass pressure.
4. If guard collapses within 5 seconds, it was not functional. Reset and try again.
5. 5 reps per guard type, then switch roles.

## Concept Transfer To Other Positions

1. Back defense: same sequence logic, safety then structure then recovery.
2. Turtle transitions: align and frame before movement.
3. Scrambles: return to sequence when chaos rises.
4. Guard retention: if retention fails, identify first broken link and repair that step first.
